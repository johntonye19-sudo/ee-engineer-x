# High-Voltage Engineering

## Fundamental Phenomena
- Electric field stress and insulation coordination
- Partial discharge (PD) and corona
- Flashover, puncture, and tracking
- Dielectric strength of air, solids, liquids, and vacuum
- Surface and volume resistivity, creepage, and clearance

## Insulation Coordination
- Define system highest voltage and overvoltage categories
- Select Basic Insulation Level (BIL) / Lightning Impulse Withstand Level
- Apply statistical or deterministic methods per IEC 60071
- Consider altitude correction and pollution severity

## Clearance & Creepage
- Follow IEC 60664 (insulation coordination for equipment within low-voltage systems) and IEC 60071 for HV
- Account for pollution degree, overvoltage category, and material group (CTI)
- Distinguish functional, basic, supplementary, and reinforced insulation

## Testing & Diagnostics
- Power-frequency withstand, lightning impulse, switching impulse
- Partial discharge measurement (IEC 60270)
- Tan-delta / power factor testing
- Insulation resistance and polarization index
- Online PD monitoring for critical assets

## Practical Design Rules
- Keep high-voltage conductors away from sharp edges and floating metal
- Control electric field with grading rings, stress cones, or capacitive grading
- Ensure adequate creepage on PCBs and insulators for the operating environment
- Design for maintainability and safe isolation/earthing points
- Never underestimate stored energy in HV capacitors or long cables
