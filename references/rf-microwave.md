# RF & Microwave Engineering

## Core Concepts
- Transmission lines (microstrip, stripline, coax, CPW): characteristic impedance, propagation constant, loss
- Reflection coefficient, VSWR, return loss, insertion loss
- S-parameters and the Smith chart
- Impedance matching networks (L, pi, T, single/double stub, quarter-wave)
- Distributed vs lumped element design

## Passive Components
- Filters (low-pass, high-pass, band-pass, band-stop) — Butterworth, Chebyshev, elliptic
- Couplers, hybrids, power dividers (Wilkinson, branch-line, rat-race)
- Circulators and isolators
- Attenuators and terminations

## Active Circuits
- RF amplifiers: gain, noise figure, linearity (P1dB, IP3), stability (Rollett K-factor, μ-factor)
- LNAs, PAs, driver stages
- Oscillators and PLLs (phase noise, spurs)
- Mixers and frequency conversion

## Antennas & Propagation (practical level)
- Basic antenna parameters: gain, directivity, efficiency, beamwidth, polarization
- Common types: dipole, monopole, patch, Yagi, horn
- Link budget fundamentals

## Layout & Practical Rules
- Controlled impedance routing and stackup impact
- Grounding and via stitching for RF
- Component placement for minimal parasitics
- Shielding and cavity resonance awareness
- Decoupling and bias networks for RF ICs

## Simulation & Measurement
- Prefer EM simulation (Method of Moments, FEM, FDTD) for distributed structures
- Network analyzer measurements, calibration (SOLT, TRL)
- Never treat ideal transmission lines as lossless at microwave frequencies without justification
