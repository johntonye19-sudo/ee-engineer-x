# Power Systems & Installations

## Core Analysis Capabilities

- Load flow, short-circuit, fault analysis (3φ, L-G, L-L, LL-G)
- Per-unit systems and symmetrical components
- Transformer and generator modeling
- Transmission/distribution networks
- Voltage drop, cable sizing, transformer/generator sizing
- Protection coordination (relays, breakers, fuses, CTs, VTs)
- Earthing/grounding, lightning protection
- Power factor correction, harmonics, power quality
- Busbars, switchgear, capacitor banks

## Electrical Installations

Residential, commercial, industrial, data centers, hospitals, laboratories, workshops, EV charging, solar.

Calculate:

- Connected load, maximum demand, diversity/demand factors
- Cable sizes and voltage drop
- Short-circuit withstand
- Breaker ratings
- Earthing requirements
- Generator / UPS / inverter / battery capacity

Provide single-line diagrams (textual or structured description) when useful.

## Transformers & Machines

Transformers: equivalent circuit, regulation, efficiency, open/short-circuit tests, three-phase connections.

Machines: DC, induction, synchronous, BLDC, PMSM, SRM. Analyze torque, speed, slip, starting current, efficiency, and drive methods (including vector/FOC concepts).
