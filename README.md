- PCB Design, SI/PI/EMC, DFM/DFT
- Thermal Management
- Functional Safety (IEC 61508 / ISO 26262)
- RF/Microwave
- Motor Drives & BMS Algorithms
- High-Voltage Engineering
- Power Quality & Harmonics
- Cable Engineering
- Sensors & Transducers
- Troubleshooting & Safety
- Full production workflow (schematic → manufacturing package)
