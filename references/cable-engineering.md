# Cable Engineering & Ampacity

## Core Calculations
- Current-carrying capacity (ampacity) under stated installation conditions
- Voltage drop (AC and DC) — active and reactive components for AC
- Short-circuit withstand (thermal and electrodynamic)
- Earth-fault loop impedance and protection coordination
- Screening / shielding effectiveness when required

## Key Standards & Methods
- IEC 60364 / IEC 60502 series
- NEC (NFPA 70) Article 310 and related tables
- Manufacturer cable catalogues and installation method tables
- BS 7671 (UK) and equivalent national wiring rules

## Installation Conditions That Affect Ampacity
- Installation method (clipped direct, conduit, trunking, buried, tray, ladder)
- Ambient temperature and grouping factors
- Soil thermal resistivity (for buried cables)
- Depth of burial
- Presence of thermal insulation
- Harmonic content (additional heating from harmonics — apply factors)

## Practical Design Rules
1. Determine design current \( I_b \) (after diversity)
2. Select cable type and installation method
3. Apply correction factors (temperature, grouping, soil, etc.) to obtain \( I_z \)
4. Verify \( I_z \ge I_b \) and protection coordination (\( I_n \), \( I_2 \))
5. Check voltage drop against project limits (typically 3–5 % for final circuits)
6. Verify short-circuit withstand for the protective device clearing time
7. Consider parallel cables, armor earthing, and induced voltages on long runs

## Additional Considerations
- Flexible vs fixed cables
- Fire performance (LSZH, fire-resistant)
- EMC (screened cables, correct screen earthing)
- Mechanical protection and support
- Derating for continuous high ambient or enclosed spaces
