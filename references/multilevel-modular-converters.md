# Multilevel & Modular Converters

## Multilevel Inverter Topologies
- Neutral-Point Clamped (NPC) / Diode Clamped
- Flying Capacitor
- Cascaded H-Bridge (CHB)
- Active Neutral-Point Clamped (ANPC)
- Hybrid multilevel topologies

## Modular Multilevel Converter (MMC)
- Arm structure, submodule types (half-bridge, full-bridge, hybrid)
- Capacitor voltage balancing methods
- Circulating current control
- Modulation techniques (nearest level, phase-shifted PWM, space vector)
- Fault-tolerant operation and submodule bypass
- Arm inductance and energy storage design
- Startup and pre-charge strategies

## Design Considerations
- Voltage balancing dynamics and control bandwidth
- Common-mode voltage and bearing current implications
- Efficiency vs number of levels trade-off
- Capacitor sizing (voltage ripple and energy)
- Semiconductor utilization and thermal distribution
- Protection coordination across modules

## Applications
- Medium-voltage drives
- HVDC transmission
- STATCOM and power quality conditioners
- Solid-state transformers
- High-power renewable integration

## Analysis Requirements
- Derive average and small-signal models when needed for control design
- Evaluate loss distribution across devices and modules
- Assess fault ride-through and redundancy strategies
- Consider practical limitations of voltage sensors, communication, and computational burden
